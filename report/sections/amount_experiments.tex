For the amount of tissue, we used the known-volume and tumour recordings with the same settings. In the known-volume files,
re-suctions were split at gaps of at least \SI{60}{\second}; the \SI{4.7}{\hour} recording was
left out because it cannot be split this way. Each recording was estimated from the others
(leave one recording out), and we report the mean error $|\hat{A} - A| / A$ of the first
suctions. To see the effect of an unknown concentration, the high and middle known volumes were
also fitted with one common $k$.
