\subsection{Detection and classification}
\label{sec:detection-discussion}
\subsubsection{Water after tissue is labelled as tissue}
All 4 water events right after the tissue in
the Big pieces recording passed the two-channel check, so water can be counted as no-dye tissue.
A shape rule could fix this: water events look noisier (AUC 0.80 against no-dye pieces).
However, most of this comes from water events being longer (length alone: AUC 0.75), and only
these 4 events show the case that matters, so a rule fitted to them would not be reliable. For the
same reason, we did not train a classifier (such as a random forest): with only 4 examples, it
would overfit. More recordings with known water after tissue are needed first.

\subsubsection{Pieces join in dense suctions}
Grouping peaks less than \SI{1}{\second} apart gives
the right number of pieces in the Big pieces recording, but when much tissue flows, as in
re-suctions, pieces join into one long event (up to about \SI{1}{\minute} in the known volumes).
The count is then too low. A shorter gap would split single pieces, which also give several
peaks within about a second. Splitting at deep valleys between peaks, where the signal returns
close to the baseline, is another option. However, without recordings of dense flow with a known
number of pieces we cannot check either rule. An indirect check would be that the Big pieces
count stays correct while the count follows the known volumes better, but with eight recordings
such a rule should be fixed before looking at the result.

\subsubsection{Fixed levels}
The no-dye level is fixed in counts, so it only holds for the gain of the
recordings it was made from; with another gain it must be measured again from a water-only
recording. The $10\sigma$ threshold also misses part of the dim signal: at middle concentration
10--27\,\% of the signal is outside the events. No whole piece was lost, so this matters only
for the area, but a lower threshold would also pick up more water noise.

\subsubsection{No \texttt{adc1}}
When \texttt{adc1} does not respond, dim events can only be
\texttt{undecided}. This needs a working second channel and cannot be fixed in software.
