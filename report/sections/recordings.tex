\subsection{Recordings}
\label{sec:recordings}
We used 29 Marginum recordings (two channels, \SI{50}{\kilo\hertz}; Table~\ref{tab:recordings}):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Big pieces}: 15 pieces each of high, middle, low and zero (no dye)
        concentration, then water. The type of every piece is known.
  \item \textbf{Water only}: no tissue, so every event is water.
  \item \textbf{Known volumes (2025)}: 1--\SI{4}{\milli\litre} at high (dye 4.86\,C) or middle
        (1.02\,C) concentration, with 0--3 re-suctions in the same file. One recording is
        \SI{4.7}{\hour} long (\SI{7.6}{\giga\byte}).
  \item \textbf{Tumour}: 1, 1, 1, 2 and \SI{3}{\gram} of tumour tissue at high concentration,
        with the re-suction in a separate file.
  \item \textbf{Demo sessions}: no known answer; used to check that the pipeline runs.
\end{itemize}
