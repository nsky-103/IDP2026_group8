\label{sec:recordings}
We used 29 Marginum recordings (two channels, \SI{50}{\kilo\hertz}):
\begin{itemize}
  \setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
  \item \textbf{Big pieces} (1 file, for the labels): 15 pieces each of high, middle, low and
        zero (no dye) concentration, then water. The type of every piece is known.
  \item \textbf{Water only} (1 file, for the labels): no tissue, so every event is water.
  \item \textbf{Known volumes (2025)} (9 files, for the amount and the speed):
        1--\SI{4}{\milli\litre} at high (dye 4.86\,C) or middle (1.02\,C) concentration, with
        0--3 re-suctions in the same file. One recording is \SI{4.7}{\hour} long
        (\SI{7.6}{\giga\byte}).
  \item \textbf{Tumour} (10 files, for the amount): 1, 1, 1, 2 and \SI{3}{\gram} of tumour tissue
        at high concentration, with the re-suction in a separate file.
  \item \textbf{Demo sessions} (8 files): no known answer; used to check that the pipeline runs.
\end{itemize}
